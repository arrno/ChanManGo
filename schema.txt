subscriber - websocket to instance riding on a worker for reading messages
publisher - websocket to instance riding on a worker for writing messages
subscription - broadcast array of subscribers on worker
message - a unit of info published to a channel
channel - a portal scoped message channel listened to by many distributes workers
portal - client domain
client - authed client
group - a permission scope for clients/channelExists
worker - pub/sub on instance hosting N client publishers/subscribers

a channel/client belongs to many groups
a portal has many clients
a client has many subscriptions/writes
a worker hosts many subscriptions/publishers
a channel is listened to by many workers
a channel has many messages
